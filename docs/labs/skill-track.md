# Skill Track

Six sequential exercises that take you from building Stave to evaluating your own AWS account. Each exercise builds on the one before it — complete them in order.

| # | Exercise                                                                 | Time   | AWS needed? |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ----------- |
| 1 | [Build & Verify](/docs/labs/skill-track/build-and-verify.md)             | 5 min  | No          |
| 2 | [First Findings](/docs/labs/skill-track/first-findings.md)               | 10 min | No          |
| 3 | [Lab Validation](/docs/labs/skill-track/lab-validation.md)               | 30 min | Sandbox     |
| 4 | [Write a Control](/docs/labs/skill-track/write-a-control.md)             | 20 min | No          |
| 5 | [Reasoning Engines](/docs/labs/skill-track/reasoning-engines.md)         | 30 min | No          |
| 6 | [Snapshot Your Account](/docs/labs/skill-track/snapshot-your-account.md) | 30 min | Read-only   |

**Who this is for:** Practitioners who have completed [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started.md) and want hands-on depth with the toolchain — building from source, validating against known-vulnerable labs, authoring controls, and running reasoning engines.
