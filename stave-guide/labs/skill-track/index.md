# Skill Track

Six sequential exercises that take you from building Stave to evaluating your own AWS account. Each exercise builds on the one before it — complete them in order.

| # | Exercise | Time | AWS needed? |
|---|----------|------|-------------|
| 1 | [Build & Verify](01-build-and-verify.md) | 5 min | No |
| 2 | [First Findings](02-first-findings.md) | 10 min | No |
| 3 | [Lab Validation](03-lab-validation.md) | 30 min | Sandbox |
| 4 | [Write a Control](04-write-a-control.md) | 20 min | No |
| 5 | [Reasoning Engines](05-reasoning-engines.md) | 30 min | No |
| 6 | [Snapshot Your Account](06-snapshot-your-account.md) | 30 min | Read-only |

**Who this is for:** Practitioners who have completed [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started) and want hands-on depth with the toolchain — building from source, validating against known-vulnerable labs, authoring controls, and running reasoning engines.
